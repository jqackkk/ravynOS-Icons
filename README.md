# ravynOS Icons
The premium and custom made icons for ravynOS.

## The goal
ravynOS strives to be unique. It has its own flair, its own style, and thus, needs its own way to convey that sense of identity. These icons have been carefully crafted through numerous revisions to ensure that each icon has the tactility of its real-world representative, yet the design and mood of a modern computer OS.

## So, what's to come?
Well, obviously, more icons. However, it all depends on the development of rayvnOS. As more and more apps begin getting supported, ravynOS-Icons as a repository will expand and grow to ensure that consistency is kept for the entire system, regardless of what computer you are using to run it. 

## What if I dislike them?
No worries. You can easily just replace the icons of the apps you want by going into Filer, right-clicking an app (.app), and then selecting "Get Info." You can then drag and drop the icon you'd like onto the icon shown in the preview, and viola! You're done.

## Learn more
You can learn more about the design of ravynOS' icons by visiting the ravynOS wiki. You can find design resources, guidelines, and general help for best practices. 

## How can I contribute?
You can contribute by:
1. Forking the repository
2. Adding your own icons you have made into a pre-existing or new folder
3. Submitting a Pull Request to the `main` branch.

Should your submission be accepted, you will have successfully contributed!
